# Logomachia

**Logomachia** (from the Greek *logos*, "word", and *machē*, "battle") is a retro pixel-art card game set in the ancient Greek agora. Collect philosophers, then make them face each other in a battle of arguments.

Created as a personal philosophy project, reworking authors and schools of thought into a game.

▶ **Play it here:** https://Brusati010.github.io/Logomachia/

## How it works

- **Open packs.** Each pack holds 5 cards. You start with 3 packs, and you win more by beating the computer.
- **Collect philosophers.** Every card has health, energy-cost arguments, a weakness, a resistance and the topics where its philosopher is strongest. Cards come in three rarities, and the rarest ones are holographic.
- **Level up.** A duplicate raises the level of that philosopher (up to level 5): more health and more damage.
- **Debate.** Pick a topic and a philosopher, then take turns. Each turn you gain energy to spend on an argument, or you reflect to gain more. Strike a weakness for double damage, but beware of resistances. The first to lose all their health loses the debate.

### Game modes

- **Against the computer**, with three difficulty levels (Easy, Normal, Hard).
- **Two players** on the same screen.

## Features

- Pixel-art interface inspired by classic tower-defense games
- Holographic and glitter effects on rare cards
- Philosophers' lines change at every attack, with no repeats until all of them have been used
- Original music for the menu and for the debate, with a volume slider in the settings
- Collection, packs and settings saved in your browser

## Philosophers

Heraclitus, Parmenides, Democritus, Protagoras, Socrates, Plato, Aristotle, Epicurus, Zeno of Citium, Augustine of Hippo. More will be added as the project grows.

## Run it locally

Download the repository and open `index.html` in any modern browser. No installation needed.
